There are 3 kinds of bindings. Strap, step-in, and carving.

  • Strap bindings are the classic binding. They are for both freestyle and freeride. They offer classic feel and flex. They are very adjustable. Many people who do tricks prefer strap bindings because of the feel.
  • Step-in bindings are the ultra fast and ultra light binding. They are also for freestyle and freeride. They work by having metal plates on the boot which then lock on to plates on the binding. This is faster and lighter than strap bindings. Some step-ins also now claim to have the feel and response of straps.
  • Carving bindings are like ski bindings. They are rigid for responsiveness and protection. These are for carving boards.
