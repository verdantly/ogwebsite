There are 3 kinds of boots. Soft-linerless, soft-w/liner, and hard.

  • Soft-linerless are boots without a liner. The padding and insulation is integrated into the boot.
  • Soft-w/liner are boots with a liner. The padding and insulation is removable and sometimes can be heated to mold to your foot.
  • Carving boots are hard and rigid for protection from the speeds of carving.

Soft boots also have a step-in version.
