There are many accessories for snowboarding. Some of them you need, most you don't really.

  • Wax. There are a couple types. Try to get snowboarding wax, and the one for the right temperature or your wax will freeze or melt. bluebirdwax
  • Leash. This is a must. It keeps you on your board. The leash attaches on your leg or boot to your bindings.
  • Tools. Tools are for emergency repairs and adjusting bindings.
  • Goggles. Keeps your eyes snow free.
  • Packs and bags. Bags are to put your board in to protect it and also store clothes. Packs are for food, extra clothing, long days, overnighters, etc.

Clothing is a big thing. You should dress in three layers.

  • Layer 1: outerlayer. This layer is to keep the elements out of your body, and to take the hard stuff out of falls. You need a good waterproof jacket and pants.
  • Layer 2: thermal. This layer keeps you warm. Fleece works best, because it is light and warm.
  • Layer 3: moisture. This layer wicks moisture from your body to keep you dry and warm. You need fabrics such as dri-fit.
  • Socks. Yeah, these are warm, and have padding in all the right areas.
