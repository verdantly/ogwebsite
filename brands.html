Snowboarding has many small brands, and some big names too. Personally, I ride a Rossignol Butane, Red Freestyle bindings, and Burton Moto boots. No one brand is the best, but many people have their favorite. Here are some good brands.

Boards:

Sims, Burton, Rossignol, K2, Arbor, Option, Lamar, Forum, Morrow, and more that I haven't heard of yet.

Bindings:

Most of these companies also make good bindings.

Boots:

Most of these companies also make good boots too.
